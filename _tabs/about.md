# Njord & Remora

## Two proposed spectrometers for the European Spallation Source

  Complimentary spectrometers Njord and Remora utilize the full ESS neutron pulse
  to expand the realm of experimental possibility and the availability of inelastic
  neutron scattering beamtime for new and existing scientists.
